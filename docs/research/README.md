# Research documentation

This template is the standing evidence checklist for research decks,
annual reviews, weekly updates, paper write-ups, and experiment
reports. Use it to ensure every claim has a hypothesis, setup, result,
interpretation, limitation, decision, and next evidence gate:
[RESEARCH_PROGRESS_FORENSIC_TEMPLATE.md](RESEARCH_PROGRESS_FORENSIC_TEMPLATE.md)

It is a coverage checklist, not slide filler, and applies alongside the
existing professor/reviewer research review gate.
